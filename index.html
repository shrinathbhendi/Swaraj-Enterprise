About Swaraj Enterprise

Pune's Trusted Surface Finishing & Plating Partner

Established in 2017, Swaraj Enterprise has grown into a leading metal finishing company in Pune, Maharashtra. We specialize in custom electroplating, electropolishing, and surface treatment solutions tailored for OEM clients.

Equipped with high-capacity semi-automatic plating lines and micro-thickness inspection labs, we serve over 500+ automotive, electrical, and precision engineering manufacturers across Maharashtra with rapid turnaround times and zero-defect quality standards.

Advanced Electroplating Tanks: Chemical baths equipped with digital temperature & current controllers.
Strict Quality Assurance: X-ray coating thickness measurement & salt spray corrosion testing.
High-Capacity & Fast Delivery: Bulk daily volume capacity with express 24-48 hr order dispatch options.
GST & Industry Compliant: Fully verified entity delivering certified industrial electroplating.
